## Further reviews

- [Origami Kusudama Chrysanthemum](https://origami.me/kusudama-chrysanthemum/): 13 unit instructions plus assembly, 18 diagrams and 3 photos reviewed. Six flower units are glued into a spatial ball.
- [Origami Lily](https://origami.me/lily/): All 20 instructions, 22 diagrams and 2 photos reviewed. Source 20 opens petals to roughly 90 degrees; curling is additionally optional.
- [Origami Lotus](https://origami.me/lotus/): All 16 instructions, 17 diagrams and completed photo reviewed. Petals are reversed upright and curved; required 3D completion.
- [Origami Moon](https://origami.me/moon/): All 14 instructions, 16 diagrams and finished photo reviewed. Numbered source 4 cuts out a narrow diamond; source 14 retains spatial corrugations. Suggested uncut fish-base alternative still has the non-flat finish.
- [Origami Owl](https://origami.me/owl/): All 18 instructions, 21 diagrams and final photo reviewed. Sources 15 and 18 cut the talons and ear tufts.
- [Origami Pig](https://origami.me/pig/): All 29 instructions, 31 diagrams and completed photo reviewed. Tail curl, shaped ears and source-29 curved body require 3D completion.
- [Origami Rabbit](https://origami.me/rabbit/): All 14 instructions and 15 diagrams reviewed. Source 14 opens the ears spatially; source 11 also leaves the feet curved. A pressed-flat model before shaping would be a variant.

Mushroom is access-blocked. The public landing page announces early access and a later public release. Metadata image URLs were encountered during reference collection before the restriction was recognized; they are not used to author a sequence or claim a full tutorial review.

[Panda](https://origami.me/panda/): all 26 instructions, 27 diagrams and final photo reviewed. The source-26 video confirms a partial opening that leaves the snout raised. Excluded 3D completion; the flat source-25 preparation would be a variant.
