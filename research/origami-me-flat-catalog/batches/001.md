## Validation and limits

- Each model has **11 native nodes, 9 numbered folding operations**, including all required tutorial folds. The pair totals 22 native nodes and 18 folding operations.
- Every state, including the initial sheet, replays identically after serializing and reopening the native sequence in a fresh public WASM engine: **24 canonical state comparisons**.
- Egg finishes with 22 faces; every exposed subface shows the colored/front paper side. Its center precrease remains visible. Shape proportions in source steps 3, 5, and 6 are approximate, and the chosen symmetric values are documented in node notes and the recipe.
- Cat Face finishes with 19 faces. Tests compare original-sheet material at both sides of the white muzzle and at the nose before and after tucking; none is obscured by the tucked flap. The loose lower point disappears and the lower edge remains at the muzzle hinge.
- These use existing UI-supported primitives: rotation, turn-over, valley, precrease, depth/layer selection, and tuck targeting. No product code or engine behavior was changed. Both completed results were inspected in the actual Fold workspace against the source illustrations. These checks support audit readiness, not a claim of exact physical or upstream parity.
- No Rust/web build was needed: this batch changes research files and public-engine recipes only. Validation used the already built bridge from the earlier tested implementation. No broad product tests were rerun.

## What the attempts taught us

**Egg:** existing flat operations suffice. No new primitive gap found. The source deliberately leaves some crease positions free; differences in outline proportions are not automatically engine defects.

**Cat Face crown:** an all-layer fold also clips both ears. Selecting the crown flap by depth preserves them. A seed placed exactly on a face seam also produced `witness_not_in_face`; moving the pick into the face resolves it. These were input mistakes, not confirmed missing operations.

**Cat Face tuck:** immediately under the visible white muzzle is the wrong pocket target. The existing engine refuses it with `tuck_infeasible` and suggests the supporting layer. Targeting the support one level beneath the white muzzle gives a unique valid tuck and preserves the visible muzzle and nose. See [before-tuck native state](../../2026-10-13-origami-fold-trials/models/archive/checkpoints/cat-face-before-tuck.osf), [wrong target and refusal](cat-face-wrong-pocket-target.json), and [accepted target/result](cat-face-tuck-probe.json). The source prose for step 7 refers to the level of step 4, while the illustration places the lower tuck at the muzzle hinge. This reconstruction follows the illustrated lower hinge and records that interpretation for audit.

**Audit UI:** custom node notes and source instruction numbers are saved, but are not shown on the current Fold step cards or Step inspector. F006 records the missing note/source mapping surface; this table is the current workaround. It does not block folding either design.
