## Dog: valid prefix and unresolved head fold

The source leaves pleat widths approximate. The chosen widths are 0.07, 0.055, and 0.06 in unit-sheet coordinates, saved in notes/recipe; these preserve the source's geometry relationships. All states through the half-fold were inspected. The white lower region before the head operation agrees with the real-paper step-12 photograph; it must not simply be recolored to match the uniformly gray rendering in diagram 13.

A source-11 depth seed placed exactly at the corner produced `witness_not_in_face`; an interior seed on the same flap resolves it. [Original refusal](dog-corner-witness-refusal.json). This is an authoring correction.

Source 13 calls for folding the front body layer left-to-right, taking its upper portion beneath the head flap without folding that flap, then repeating behind. The vertical crease is referenced to the head pleat, and the required step-14 lowering depends on these folds.

The depth-one valley attempt succeeds as an operation but its connected moving block includes the head. The corresponding tuck refuses with `tuck_target_moves` (face 24). A tentative folded-edge swivel pick instead yields `swivel_not_a_fold`; that pick does not establish a valid compound request. These outcomes **do not prove that the engine lacks a suitable operation**. Layer/crease selection and compound-fold interpretation remain unresolved.
